**"Who Can It Be Now?”**

[[Olivia Raith|Olivia]] talks to her family, where they reveal their vampiric heritage and their views on humans. Olivia is not exactly a fan. ([[White Court]])

The rest of the party is talking to [[Hazel Hayes]], Warden [[Donald Morgan]], and Warden Captain [[Anastasia Luccio]] about the threats currently plaguing the city - a warlock using black magic to blow the hearts out of chests and someone is creating and spreading a drug that allows regular humans to access [[The Sight]], which is bad because you remember everything you see under the Sight forever and it easily drives people insane.

[[Wardens of the White Council|The Wardens]] leave to do their own investigating into the matter, and [[Donald Morgan]] gives his condolences to [[Arthur Sinclair|Arthur]] for his brother and father. The party has some pizza and then parts ways to rest for the night after a frantic day, and prepare for a race against the storm that will kill [[Rachael Robins]] tomorrow if they don’t find the warlock who took her hair.

First things first - after waking up, the party meets [[Rachael Robins|Rachael]]’s main contact [[Lewis Graham Monroe]] at a McDonalds. [[Olivia Raith|Olivia]] orders one of everything on the menu, and Lewis Graham Monroe says he will start tracking the storms. [[Sadie Cooper|Sadie]] uses her [[The Sight|Sight]] on the food (the soulless, corporate breakfast sandwiches that will haunt her memories forever) and looks at the party after. 

[[Arthur Sinclair|Arthur]] looks like a weary Roman soldier, with blood trickling out of where his heart is. [[Rachael Robins|Rachael]] looks mostly like herself, but icy frost is starting to form on her fingers. [[Olivia Raith|Olivia]] looks bright and cheery but a slow burning, dark purple blaze is centered in her core. [[Lewis Graham Monroe]]’s soul is just him with a tinfoil hat.

[[Lewis Graham Monroe]] helps rationalize magic as Alien Powers to [[Rachael Robins|Rachael]], and then says he will do his best to locate where the ritual is being performed by seeing where the storm clouds were at the times of death for the victims. The party departs our dear Lewis Graham Monroe as he heads home to his super sick setup.

After leaving McDonalds with a plastic sack full of mostly contained and uneaten breakfast foods, the party heads off to their next contact - [[Susan Rodriguez]] of the [[Midwest Arcane]], a reporter who actually knows about the magical world. Susan gives as much information as she can in return for the promise of a story from Olivia. She doesn’t know much of the storms, but knows about the drugs and how its definitely not [[Marcone]] - since he’s losing territory in a power struggle. Assumes its one of Marcone’s lieutenants.

With the storm approaching and running out of time, [[Arthur Sinclair|Arthur]] takes the party to [[Madame Bianca]]’s, local [[Red Court]] vampire with some measure of influence. She has *someone* perform a magical spell to locate where the ritual is taking place with a lock of Rachael’s hair, and speaks to Arthur with a hint of recognition. 

Taking [[Madame Bianca]]’s boat and racing against the ever approaching storm clouds, the party storms what they later discover to be [[Victor Sells]]’ Lakehouse. Interrupting the ritual that would have killed [[Rachael Robins|Rachael]], [[Olivia Raith|Olivia]] eats someone’s emotions and psychic energy so hard they die and she becomes a full [[White Court]] vampire, Rachael learns she can go invisible, and after killing all of his co-conspirators while they were psychically linked to one another Victor goes insane. The [[Wardens of the White Council]] arrive a little late, which really isn’t their fault because nobody warned them what was up until about 30 seconds before the party started kicking ass. [[Sadie Cooper|Sadie]] shot someone to death with a gun, as is her god given American right.

The Wardens take [[Victor Sells]] away, promising to bring Rachael for the interrogation before the execution due to the crime of breaking one of the laws of magic. The party then leaves the house and waits for their uber, with promises of entwined futures on the horizon.
