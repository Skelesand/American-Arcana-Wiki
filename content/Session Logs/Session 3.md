Session 3: “Sharp Dressed Man”

-2 months passes for our group, and each person spent the following time in different ways.
Sadie has officially joined the White Council, and has been practicing her transforming magic under Hazel’s guidance. There have been some…missteps along the way, but especially for a mostly self taught wizard Sadie is taking to it remarkably well.

-Olivia has been trying to keep her grades up, but is pretty depressed because a literal demon lives inside of her. Hangs out with Thomas when she can, and keeps up appearances with her family.

-Rachael has accepted work with Johnny Marcone, and her life has actually improved pretty greatly. Runs experiments with Lewis Graham Monroe to see the limits of her ~~alien powers~~ magic, and has probably electrocuted herself once or twice.

-Arthur has been spending the last couple months helping teach the girls on the weekends, and at night hunts the ever increasingly present Red Court vampires with his new friend Michael Carpenter, a genuine Catholic Paladin of the modern day.

-Throughout the morning of October 27th, all 3 of the girls through various circumstances are invited to a Red Court Ball on the 31st, for reasons unknown. Sadie and Hazel will be representing the White Council, Olivia and Thomas Raith will be representing the White Court, and Rachael is the plus one of the Leanansidhe of the Winter Court.

-Raymond Sinclair, forcibly retired monster hunter and Arthur’s father has come to Chicago both to visit his last son and to warn him - the Reds are up to something, and it can’t be good. Various artifacts are being shipped in for *something* that will be happening on the 31st, either connected to or directly with the Ball.

-He only knows of the first one, which is a Spirit of Intellect - an incredibly smart spiritual super computer with no moral compass - which the Reds absolutely cannot be allowed to control. He believes it is currently being shipped to Chicago via the “hidden in plain sight” philosophy - through Amazon.

-The party gets access to the warehouse through various means - Olivia directly asks a young nerd for his clothes and ID, Arthur tricks an old lady by saying she won a prize, Sadie talks an blue collar man by saying this job sucks ass anyway, and Rachael is just a regular smooth talking criminal who finds the smallest person she can.

-Sadie discovers she can focus her magical energies to make electrical objects break - but she doesn’t have full mastery of this yet. Lightbulbs got-got but the camera’s got hands.

-Using her Detect Magic, Sadie leads the party through the warehouse where she eventually finds a wooden box holding what the party can only assume is the Spirit of Intellect - but while they’re debating what to do about it, Michael calls with a warning that the Reds are here and entering the building. Either they followed you here or they were waiting for a moment to strike.

-At that moment all the lights go out, doors are thrown open, and loud inhuman shrieks echo through the warehouse. Ready or not, the Reds are here to play.
