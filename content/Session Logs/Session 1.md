**"m.A.A.d City"**

After the recent deaths at their college, [[Sadie Cooper]] and [[Olivia Raith]] are riding in the back of Lt. [[Karrin Murphy]]'s car to head the Special Investigations' building in order to give their statements, when their car was attached by The Shadow Man, who Sadie instantly shoots.

With a destroyed engine and a wrecked front windshield, the car crashes directly into a building with the only way out being an alleyway which itself contains a state of mayhem as [[Arthur Sinclair]] and [[Rachael Robins]] (with soup cans and crowbar in hand) are attacking weirdly not-quite-humanoid [[Goop Gangsters]].

The Shadow Man becomes the Three Shadow Men and form a wall and the [[Goop Gangsters]] appear to be endlessly pouring out of a dumpster through a portal to the [[Nevernever]]. Despite various heroics such as Arthur going Popeye Mode and Sadie destroying 5 goop men then knocking herself out, the party is being worn down.

At this moment, The [[Leanansidhe]] appears and offers to save them all in return for a favor. Arthur agrees, and she allows the 4 of you + Murphy to follow her into the [[Nevernever]], but not before 3 of the Goop Gangsters manage to take 3 fistfuls of [[Rachael Robins |Rachael's]] hair.

After some walking and talking and various revelations of [[Rachael Robins|Rachael's]] past, the [[Leanansidhe]] is promised favors owed from both [[Olivia Raith]] (for healing Sadie) and [[Sadie Cooper]] (to let them out). She reveals she cannot be owed a favor from Rachael, and grants her a small boon before everyone leaves.

After the [[Leanansidhe]] opens [[The Way]], you realize that she has both taken you to [[Olivia Raith]]'s familial home, [[The Raith Estate]], and you've lost roughly 12 hours. [[Karrin Murphy]] immediately leaves to go back to the police station with nobody noticing and the four of them go towards [[Olivia Raith|Olivia's]] backyard, where her 20th birthday and absurd display of opulence awaits.

[[Lord Raith]] appears to recognize [[Arthur Sinclair|Arthur]], and Arthur appears to get extremely guarded. [[Olivia Raith|Olivia]] meets with her family which includes a loving if not weird father, a somewhat distant sister, and a drunk but very worried brother who immediately tells her to get her friends out of there. [[Rachael Robins|Rachael]] realizes that [[Sadie Cooper|Sadie]] has a dog and gives the billionaire $20 for her birthday.

[[Arthur Sinclair|Arthur]], [[Sadie Cooper|Sadie]], and [[Rachael Robins|Rachael]] take an Uber to Sadie's apartment where [[Hazel Hayes]] (a wizard) is waiting for them, as well as two [[Wardens of the White Council]] - [[Donald Morgan]] and [[Anastasia Luccio]]. Sadie is told that they need to talk, and Morgan appears to recognize Arthur.
