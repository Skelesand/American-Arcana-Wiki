**"The Monster Mash"**

The party rolls up to, well, [[Red Court Ball|The Party]]. They meet up and then split apart to mingle with the pringles a bit, not before being mocked for looking kinda goofy by some weirdly dressed Louisiana accent duo, a man and a woman.

The [[Leanansidhe]] drags [[Rachael Robins|Rachael]] around to various people, both representatives of the [[Summer Court]] ([[Ronald Reuel]] the [[Summer Knight]] and the [[Summer Lady]]) and then to a man introduced as [[Vadderung]] (who Leah specifically asked "who are you today?" today). The [[Leanansidhe]] didn't direct Rachael much at all, almost appearing to be showing her off than actually introducing her to anyone.

[[Michael Carpenter|Michael]] stomps off towards two who he refers to as [[Denarians]], naming one [[Nicodemus]] and the other [[Deirdre]]. Michael looks nearly homicidal when [[Nicodemus]] casual threatens to go to his home, but manages to back off. [[Arthur Sinclair|Arthur]] is nearby in case things go south

[[Sadie Cooper|Sadie]] eavesdrops on the [[Svartalves]] who mention that they built the house up from what it used to be to the giant manor it is now, and are owed payment. She then went to go talk to [[Ferrovax]], an extremely powerful being that (as far as I remember) nobody actually knows what he is. He is very old and likes gold. Gets invited to the party to not fuck it up.

[[Olivia Raith|Olivia]] goes to talk to [[The Archive]], an 8 year old girl with two body guards who claims to be the collection of all human knowledge. The guards are protective of her. She then walks down towards 2 nazgul looking motherfuckers in the corner but before she actually talks to them sees [[Binder]] and goes to him.

[[Binder]] tells her to gather everyone, and they sneak off to a separate room. He tells her that  her dad hired him to look for and help [[Olivia Raith|Olivia]] and [[Thomas Raith|Thomas]]. Olivia was given a gun from Binder, who *claimed* her dad wanted her to have it. He then tells them that he knows where their allies are being held, which is in [[Madame Bianca]]'s basement. He said he will go cause a distraction to give them time. Nobody knows it but Binder and several of his goons started a giance Cha Cha Slide.

[[Sadie Cooper|Sadie]] uses Pass Without Trace, giving the group (we seriously need a name for you guys) an easy way through the mass of bodies and into the basement, where fighting breaks out. The group manages to free [[Thomas Raith|Thomas]] and [[Hazel Hayes|Hazel]]. Hazel had anti-magic cuffs on that Sadie removes, and Olivia catches a bullet and parkours over like 4 dudes to save Thomas.

[[Raymond Sinclair|Raymond]] is nowhere to be found, and before the group is able to get themselves and their allies out of there, a familiar voice to [[Arthur Sinclair|Arthur]] is heard from the top of the basement stairs  [[Marcus Sinclair]] is here, and he knows they are as well.
