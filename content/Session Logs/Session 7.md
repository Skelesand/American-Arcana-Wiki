**"Just the Two of Us"**

[[Arthur Sinclair|Arthur]] and [[Olivia Raith|Olivia]] meet [[Binder]] at [[Brindille]], where they discuss payment. Binder is clearly holding out and looking for payment, which makes Arthur mad. At one point in the conversation he phases out mentally for a second, with the lunch party being unaware he is looking through and controlling one of his goons.

[[Sadie Cooper|Sadie]] and [[Rachael Robins|Rachael]] make their way to his rented apartment, where Sadie walks around as a gecko and Rachael waits in the hallway. Sadie makes her way through the apartment spotting a decent amount of Binder's Goons waiting perfectly still where he left them. 

When she reaches the bedroom, she finds an old type writer and 3 folders of notes. One is titled [[Madame Bianca|Bianca]], one is titled [[Mavra]], and one is titled [[Marcus Sinclair|Marcus]]. She makes her way to the closet where she finds an old locked chest, which upon opening it is when [[Binder]] comes in and basically says "Hey man its a nice try but I've dealt with shapeshifitng geckos before. I have to try to kill you, if you make it out all's fair"

[[Rachael Robins|Rachael]] and [[Sadie Cooper|Sadie]] try to escape. Sadie is mostly grazed by 20 or 30 tommy gun bullets, while Rachael is shot 97 times and falls unconscious. With the help of a fire chicken they escape.

Arthur commands Binder to release his men, and Binder says he cant being so far away.

After escaping, Rachael and Sadie meet up with the group at Brindille. Rachael receives some magical healing, but seems to be being assisted in some way by the fae. 

The group talks business with [[Binder]], who is pretty casual about the whole thing. They return the journal to him so as far as he's concerned, nothing personal was crossed and it was all business. Binder tell the party he already gave the coin to someone, and then told them the names Sadie saw. Reveals [[Mavra]] is a [[Black Court]] vampire and a wizard, and tells [[Arthur Sinclair|Arthur]] that [[Marcus Sinclair|Marcus]] is back in town.

After this, the party splits to do research. The girls don't learn much about Marcus, but they learn that [[Mavra]] is an extremely old black court vampire. They also meet [[Justine]], an extremely creative and nearly bottomless well of psychic energy woman who is Thomas' girlfriend.

Arthur tells [[Father Foothill]] about the coin, and Foothill seems to have been expecting it to go that way. Arthur then goes home and learns that Mavra is mostly a hit and run fighter, and that the magic she's been learning mostly focuses on evocation, enchantment, illusion, and necromancy. 

The party receives two magic items - the [[Duffel Bag of Holding]] and the [[Ring of Jumping]]

With new information learned, various ascension rituals the party has dismissed may now be back on the table. With nothing else to do for tonight, the girls go costume shopping and everyone then crashes at Arthur's, waiting for word from [[Susan Rodriguez]] tomorrow on where the track team may be.
