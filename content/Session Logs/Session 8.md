**"Life Is ~~finally~~ A Highway"**

Arty and The Girls meet [[Susan Rodriguez]] and [[Karrin Murphy]] at a Burger King, where you can have it your way. Susan and Murph figured out a biker gang of werewolves called the [[Streetwolves]] kidnapped the track team, and their main hideout is an abandoned factory turned garage turned biker doghome in [[Gary, Indiana]].

Susan stays in Chicago (and as a reporter, is probably up to no good) while Murphy drives the crew to [[Gary, Indiana|Gary]]. A bit of small talk occurs, while [[Sadie Cooper|Sadie]] spends the drive as a frog fucking with the radio, managing to torture the crew with Radioactive by Imagine Dragons at some point.

After arriving at the scene, the group sneaks up to the garage and is surprised by what they see - no werewolves in the building, but a couple of [[Red Court]]s, 5 thralls, and [[Mavra]] the [[Black Court]]. Mavra opens a *massive* portal to the [[Nevernever]] in the wall of the garage and then leaves. The Reds drive through it with a semi truck full of the track team werewolves in cages.

Hopping in a truck and flooring it, the group catches up. After an intense fight through an ever changing [[Nevernever]] highway on top of the back of a semi, and several times falling off of it and only being saved by a magic chicken, the group commandeers the semi.

Now stuck in the Nevernever with a team of werewolves, the crew must figure out how to escape and get back to the mortal world before too much time passes - minutes in the Nevernever can be hours in the real world, and the Halloween deadline is fast approaching
