**"What Day Is It, October?"**

The party helps the werewolves escape, and learn they don't know much of the [[Nevernever]]. To escape, [[Rachael Robins|Rachael]] summons the [[Leanansidhe]]. She helps guide all of you out of the Nevernever through various ways and gets you all back to [[The University of Chicago]]

Once you're back in cell service, you all get a plethora of texts that leads to several revelations. In totality you learn [[Raymond Sinclair]], [[Thomas Raith]], and [[Hazel Hayes]] are missing and have tried to go rescue you from the [[Red Court]] after you guys went missing for two days.

[[Olivia Raith]] and [[Lara Raith]] bond slightly more, with a shared feeling of their brother being a *fucking* idiot. Olivia mentions offering the Red Court [[Bob]] the [[Spirit of Intellect]]

[[Arthur Sinclair|Arthur]] learns from [[Toot-toot]] that the [[Winter Court]] and the [[Summer Court]] aren't really *friends* and have warred in the past. The [[Winter Court]] is logic, violence, darkness - the brutality of Winter. The [[Summer Court]] is growth, passion, love - the warmth of Summer

[[Rachael Robins|Rachael]] has some fun interactions with Fae, and we'll get no more into that! Except that Rachael directly asks the [[Leanansidhe]] "Can we really do anything? Won't this start a war?" to which the Leanansidhe replied "Isn't that the point of free will?"

The Leanansidhe gathers Sadie, Arthur, Rachael, and [[Michael Carpenter|Michael]] in a genuine pumpkin carriage and set off at 100mph towards [[Bianca's Estate]], which has grown much larger in the last 2 months. 

Everyone arrives at about the same time to the party, and the Party quickly realizes they are the only ones in costumes. Fuck the Red Court and their stupid dress rules
